Welcome to **Cosmo**, a modern and user-friendly café menu application designed to enhance your dining experience. This web application allows customers to browse the menu, select items, and easily calculate their total bill before placing an order.

## Introduction

Cosmo is the perfect digital companion for any café-goer. It provides a seamless way to explore the café’s offerings, add items to your cart, and see the total cost of your order in real-time. Whether you're dining in or ordering to go, Cosmo Pablo makes the process simple and enjoyable.

Visit the live site: [Cosmo](https://cosmopablo.netlify.app/)

## Features

- **Interactive Menu**: Browse through a variety of food and drink items with detailed descriptions and prices.
- **Cart System**: Add items to your cart and easily adjust quantities as needed.
- **Bill Calculation**: Automatically calculate the total cost of your order, including any applicable taxes.
- **Responsive Design**: Optimized for both desktop and mobile users, ensuring a smooth experience on any device.
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Intuitive design that makes ordering fast and easy.

## Technologies

- **HTML5 & CSS3**: For building the structure and styling of the web pages.
- **JavaScript / TypeScript**: For implementing interactivity and dynamic bill calculations.
- **React**: A robust JavaScript library for managing the user interf
- **Netlify**: For deployment and hosting of the web application.
